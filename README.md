# Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics Internship

### Task 1 – Business Sales Performance Analytics

## 1. Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship – Task 1.

The objective of this project is to analyze business sales data and identify sales trends, top-performing products, category performance, regional performance, and profitability patterns.

The analysis was performed using the Superstore Sales dataset and Microsoft Power BI.

## 2. Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze sales and revenue trends over time
- Identify top-selling products
- Compare sales performance across product categories
- Compare sales performance across regions
- Analyze profit performance
- Identify important business insights
- Provide actionable recommendations based on the analysis

## 3. Dataset

Dataset used:

Superstore Sales Dataset

The dataset contains information related to:

- Orders
- Order dates
- Products
- Categories
- Regions
- Sales
- Profit
- Quantity
- Customers

## 5. Data Cleaning and Preparation

The dataset was imported into Power BI and prepared using Power Query.

The following checks and transformations were performed:

- Checked for missing values
- Checked for errors
- Reviewed data types
- Checked the structure of the dataset
- Verified relevant columns
- Created a Month-Year field for time-series analysis
- Created a Month-Year sorting field
- Prepared the data for dashboard analysis

The dataset did not require artificial insertion of missing values because the relevant data was already available.

## 9. Key Business Insights

- Technology is the leading category in both sales and profit.
- West is the strongest region in terms of both sales and profit.
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier is the top-selling product.
- Sales show noticeable month-to-month fluctuations over the analysis period.
- Central region and lower-profit categories represent areas where the business can explore improvement opportunities.

## 10. Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing Technology products and maintain adequate inventory.
- Monitor and promote top-selling products to reduce the risk of stock-outs.
- Investigate the reasons for lower performance in the Central region.
- Analyze customer demand, pricing, and product mix in weaker-performing areas.
- Use historical sales trends to improve inventory and promotional planning.
