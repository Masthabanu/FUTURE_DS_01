# Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics Internship

### Task 1 – Business Sales Performance Analytics

## 1. Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship – Task 1.

The objective of this project is to analyze business sales data and identify:

- Monthly Sales Trend
- Top-selling products
- Category performance
- Regional performance
- Profitability patterns

The analysis was performed using the Superstore Sales dataset and Microsoft Power BI.

## 2. Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze sales trends over time
- Identify top-selling products
- Compare sales performance across product categories
- Compare sales performance across regions
- Analyze profit performance
- Identify important business insights
- Provide actionable recommendations based on the analysis

## 3. Dataset

Superstore Sales Dataset

## 4. Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query

## 5. Data Cleaning and Preparation

The dataset was imported into Power BI and prepared using Power Query.

The following steps were performed:

- Checked for missing values
- Checked for errors
- Reviewed data types
- Checked the structure of the dataset
- Verified relevant columns
- Prepared the data for dashboard analysis

The dataset did not require artificial insertion of missing values because the relevant data was already available.

## 6. Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard contains the following KPIs:

- Total Sales: 2.30M
- Total Profit: 286.40K
- Total Orders: 5,009
- Total Quantity: 38K

## 7. Dashboard Analysis

### Monthly Sales Trend Analysis

A monthly sales trend was created to understand how sales changed over time.

### Top Product Analysis

The Top 10 Products by Sales were identified to understand which products generated the highest sales.

The **Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier** is the highest-selling product in the analysis.

### Sales by Category

Sales performance was analyzed across:

- Technology
- Furniture
- Office Supplies

**Technology** is the strongest category in terms of sales.

### Profit by Category

Profitability was compared across the three categories.

**Technology** generates the highest profit, while **Furniture** has comparatively lower profit performance.

### Sales by Region

Sales were compared across:

- West
- East
- Central
- South

The **West region** has the highest sales performance.

### Profit by Region

Regional profitability was also analyzed.

The **West region** has the highest profit performance among the four regions.

Interactive slicers were also added for:

- Year
- Category
- Region

## 8. Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis, the following insights were identified:

### 1. Technology is the strongest category

Technology performs strongly in both **sales and profit**, making it an important category for the business.

### 2. West is the strongest region

The **West region** records the highest sales and profit among the four regions.

### 3. Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier is the top-selling product

The **Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier** ranks first among the analyzed products by sales.

### 4. Sales fluctuate over time

The monthly sales trend shows considerable variation across different months and years, indicating changing sales patterns over time.

### 5. Furniture has comparatively lower profitability

Although Furniture contributes to overall sales, its profit performance is lower compared with Technology and Office Supplies.

# 9. Actionable Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following recommendations can be considered:

### 1. Focus on high-performing Technology products

The business should continue supporting the Technology category through effective inventory management, promotions, and product availability.

### 2. Monitor top-selling products

High-demand products such as the **Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier** should be monitored carefully to reduce the possibility of stock shortages.

### 3. Maintain strong performance in the West

Since West is the strongest region, the business should maintain its successful sales strategies while exploring opportunities to increase customer value.

### 4. Investigate lower-performing regions

Lower-performing regions should be studied further to understand differences in customer demand, product mix, pricing, discounts, and sales strategies.

### 5. Improve Furniture profitability

The business should investigate Furniture-level pricing, discounts, costs, and product performance to identify opportunities to improve margins.

### 6. Use sales trends for business planning

Historical sales patterns can be used to improve inventory planning, promotional campaigns, and resource allocation during periods of higher and lower demand.

## 10. Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of data cleaning, KPI analysis, visualization, business intelligence, and data-driven decision-making using Power BI.

The dashboard provides a clear view of sales and profitability performance and helps identify areas where the business can focus its growth and improvement efforts.
